# PAP devices: case plan

Six cases against `data/policies/pap-sleep-apnea.yaml` (LCD L33718 v52, article A52467 v54), labeled under `evals/GUIDELINES.md`. Each case changes one or two facts from a clean baseline; every other scored criterion is `met`, or `not_applicable` where its condition does not hold. The policy has no request-stage criteria, so every label rests on the chart. The request block carries only the dates, the plain-words procedure and the code that routes the variant (`E0601` or `E0470`); it has no technique, region or levels.

## Template patient

The shared background patient (`evals/template/patient.json`), aged 65, with no sleep, hypertension, stroke or insomnia history in the background. It has one fact that bears on a criterion, see the first limitation below.

Clean first-device history (`initial`):

- In-person sleep medicine evaluation on 2026-08-24 by the treating physician, with snoring, witnessed pauses in breathing and excessive daytime sleepiness documented.
- The same physician orders a Type III home sleep apnea test at that visit (a ServiceRequest and the note record the order). The test is on 2026-09-02, AHI 32.1 events per hour (230 events over 7 h 10 min), in a free-text report and a coded Observation. Results visit on 2026-09-10 prescribes a CPAP device (E0601).
- Request on 2026-10-05, `service_date` 2026-10-19 in every case.

The index is computed by the build script from the event count and the study length, rounded to one decimal.

## Design notes

- **pap-03:** the nightly usage is in one written report note and is generated by `evals/build_cases.py`, which fails unless the best consecutive 30-night period is exactly 20 nights of 4 hours or more (21 would be exactly 70%). Nights count at 4.6 hours or more; the others are 3.4 or less. The report also gives average use (4.0 hours per night) and nights with any use (86 of 92), which are not the criterion. A test checks that the report chunks keep the 20-of-30 statement and that the nightly values in the chunks reproduce it.
- **pap-03 `sleep-test-type`:** the label rests on the facility-based polysomnogram wording, "a polysomnogram performed in a facility-based laboratory (Type I study)", not the home-test wording.
- **pap-04:** the telephone note documents failure on CPAP, interface fit and pressure settings without dating the CPAP use, so those three labels are `met` on either reading. If the E0601 was used for more than 3 months, the required new in-person evaluation is absent (the order was by telephone and no visit follows); if not, the criterion is not applicable. The record cannot settle which, so GUIDELINES rule 4 gives `insufficient`.
- **pap-06:** GUIDELINES rule 3 is written for criteria phrased as an absence; both adherence criteria are positive requirements, so the Outcomes table decides. `not_met` needs evidence that the criterion is not satisfied, and nothing shows the line was missed. The patient's own statement that the mask is worn most nights is not objective evidence. `insufficient` fits. A note saying that no download was obtained would make `adherence-report-in-record` `not_met` (rule 5); this record does not say so.
- **Boundaries avoided (rule 2):** AHI values stay clear of the undefined gap between 14 and 15 (32.1, 28.4, 26.7, 11.8, 3.4); E0601 use is about 8 months or about 7 weeks, never 3 months; the re-evaluation is on day 61, not at the day 31 or 91 edges, where the day count is not defined; no short study, because "with symptoms" is not defined; no night lies between 3.4 and 4.6 hours.
- **LOINC:** the index is recorded as text only. The one total AHI code on the LOINC terminology server, 69990-0, is defined over 24 hours. 28633-6, "Polysomnography (sleep) study", was confirmed and codes the polysomnogram report.

## Known limitations

- **`osa-symptom-or-condition` is met in every PAP case, so its not-met path is untested.** The shared background patient has a coded active ischemic heart disease Condition (diagnosed 2026-07-21), which the criterion names. The cases keep that background on purpose, so the facet and PAP sets share one patient. Every case also documents daytime sleepiness, but a chart with neither a listed symptom nor a listed condition cannot be built on this template.
- The short-study criterion is `not_applicable` in all six cases; the case where the condition holds is not covered.
- Evaluation after the sleep test, a late re-evaluation, and E0471 (not a variant) are not covered.
- All PAP cases share one background patient, and the same patient as the facet cases.
- Single annotator; not clinically validated.

## Checks before a case is accepted

- Every changed label cites the quoted sentence it follows from.
- Every scored criterion in the variant has a label.
- The overall outcome computed from the labels matches the table.
- The bundle contains the evidence the labels cite, with the resource ids listed.
